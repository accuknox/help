## Title

Alternate: *How RBI Model Risk Management Requirements Map to AI and ML Security Controls for Banks and NBFCs*

## Subtitle

**A requirement-by-requirement map of the RBI's Model Risk Management guidance to the AI and ML security controls your institution can deploy, with a clear line on what stays with your Model Risk Management Framework.**

Alternate: *What the RBI guidance asks of your AI and ML models, the security control that meets each requirement, and where your own responsibility begins.*

## What's inside, and who it's for

- Every AI and ML requirement in the RBI guidance, matched to the security control that meets it
- A clear line between what your Model Risk Management Framework owns and what security tooling covers
- One consolidated table covering the requirement, the AccuKnox capability, and the coverage (Direct, Supports, or Yours)
- A four-step rollout that follows RBI's own risk order
- How the controls run on-premises so model data stays inside the bank
- Architecture and flow diagrams in the appendix

**Written for** CISOs, heads of model risk, and the security, risk, and model-validation teams putting AI into production at RBI-regulated entities, from commercial and co-operative banks to NBFCs, payments and small finance banks, all-India institutions like NABARD and EXIM Bank, and credit information companies.
