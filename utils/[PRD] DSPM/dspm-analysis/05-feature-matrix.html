Scoring Method

This matrix scores public evidence, not private product completeness. A vendor gets ✅ Strong when the capability is explicit, clearly demonstrated, or visually reinforced. ⚠️ Partial means the capability is implied or present but not strongly evidenced. ❌ Missing means it was not visibly supported in the sampled public materials.

Strategy Note

What AccuKnox should and should not chase

Chase immediately

  • Landing dashboard proof density
  • Identity-aware exposure analysis
  • RBI, DPDP, and TRAI compliance cockpit
  • Remediation command center and closure evidence
  • Correlated risk with cloud and runtime posture

Do not chase in the first wave

  • BigID-level governance sprawl across every adjacent category
  • Varonis-level product surface breadth across every mature module
  • Excessive feature parity checklists without narrative differentiation
  • AI messaging without visible AI-specific screens or controls
