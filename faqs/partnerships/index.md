    - **Technology Partners**: AccuKnox has alliances with Nutanix, IBM Cloud, NVIDIA and others for integrations and enhanced product offerings.
    - **System Integrators & Distributors**: SIs, GSIs and distributors deliver customized AccuKnox solutions to customers. Examples include Wipro, NTT Data, TCS and Carahsoft.

??? "**4. How does the AccuKnox's MSSP model work?**"
    MSSPs use the AccuKnox platform to **manage and secure multiple customers across different clouds** from one dashboard.

    - **Isolation**: Each client gets isolated access.
    - **Policies**: Each client gets custom policies.
    - **Onboarding**: MSSPs onboard new clients easily.
    - **Growth**: MSSPs can brand, automate and scale their services.
    - **Operations**: MSSPs monitor, bill and support all customers from one dashboard.

    Read more about our [MSSP partnership program](https://accuknox.com/blog/mssp-ready-cnapp).

??? "**5. Can MSSP users access end customer tenant accounts?**"
    Yes. MSSP users can get **viewer or editor access to end customer tenants**, with customer permission. MSSP models need this access, and the AccuKnox platform supports it securely.
