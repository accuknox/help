The highest-leverage features for AccuKnox MVP demo and enterprise buying confidence.

Prioritization Rule

The 80/20 rule for this roadmap

Priority goes to the smallest set of screens and data flows that change competitive perception fastest. That means we are not optimizing for total feature parity. We are optimizing for a materially stronger first demo, stronger executive confidence, and a clearer differentiated wedge for regulated cloud-native enterprises.

If a feature does not improve one of these four moments, it is not in the first wave: first landing page, first critical finding investigation, first remediation action, first audit review.
Top 10

1. Unified sensitive data risk dashboard

Why it matters: This is the first impression. It must immediately compete with Cyera's dashboard and Sentra's executive-friendly posture surfaces.

Buyer outcome: Executive sponsor sees overall risk, top business units, critical assets, and urgent actions within seconds.

2. Identity-aware exposure graph

Why it matters: Access is now a buying-center question, not a niche analyst concern.

Buyer outcome: Security teams understand who can reach sensitive data, through which path, and where access is excessive.

3. Explainable risk prioritization

Why it matters: A finding without explanation feels like scanner noise.

Buyer outcome: Analysts trust why an issue is ranked high and can defend prioritization decisions internally.

4. One-click plus workflow remediation

Why it matters: The category is judged on closure, not only discovery.

Buyer outcome: Remediation can be executed, delegated, approved, and evidenced from inside the console.

5. RBI, DPDP, and TRAI compliance cockpit

Why it matters: This creates a meaningful regional wedge for large Indian enterprises.

Buyer outcome: Risk and compliance stakeholders see obligations, failing controls, and exportable evidence in one place.

6. Lineage and access timeline

Why it matters: Investigation quality is now a competitive benchmark because of Cyera and Varonis.

Buyer outcome: Analysts can answer who touched the data, when, how, and what changed over time.

7. Continuous drift monitoring

Why it matters: Buyers want posture to look alive, not frozen.

Buyer outcome: Changes in access, classification, exposure, and compliance status are visible as movement, not static snapshots.

8. Executive report automation

Why it matters: Board and steering-committee communication needs a no-drama path.

Buyer outcome: A sponsor can export a clean, role-specific summary without manually rebuilding the story in slides.

9. GCP-first connector experience

Why it matters: Airtel-style environments may care about GCP depth as much as AWS visibility.

Buyer outcome: Architectural confidence rises when connector posture and scan readiness are explicitly visible.

10. CNAPP x DSPM correlated risk

Why it matters: This is the most defensible strategic differentiator against pure-play DSPM vendors.

Buyer outcome: High-risk findings can be ranked by actual exploitability and environment context, not only data sensitivity.

Sequencing

Recommended release sequence

Wave 1: Demo credibility

  • Risk dashboard
  • Explainable prioritization
  • Executive report export
  • GCP-first connector visibility

Wave 2: Investigation strength

  • Identity-aware exposure graph
  • Asset detail and access timeline
  • Continuous drift monitoring

Wave 3: Differentiated action

  • Workflow remediation
  • RBI, DPDP, and TRAI cockpit
  • CNAPP correlated risk
What to Cut

De-prioritize these until the flagship story is sharp

  • Excessive long-tail connector polish before the main onboarding and posture experience is great.
  • Broad governance-category expansion that dilutes the DSPM wedge.
  • Too many new dashboards instead of one excellent landing dashboard with strong drilldowns.
  • AI claims without visible AI-specific evidence or control surfaces.
