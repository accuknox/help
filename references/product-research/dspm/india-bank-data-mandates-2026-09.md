# Indian Bank Data Mandates for DSPM

The RBI customer data protection advisory of 25 March 2026 is the lead hook. It asks banks to use
automated tools to tag and classify customer data, and that is what DSPM does. It is an advisory, so
call it "an RBI advisory" and never "an RBI regulation" or "a mandate".

All sources below are external. Link the primary source on any public page. Where only a secondary
source exists, the row says so.

## Four Items Give a Bank a Reason to Buy DSPM in 2026

| Item | Type | Date | What it asks, as it bears on DSPM | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RBI Advisory No. 3/2026, Best Practices Relating to Customer Data Protection | Advisory from the RBI Department of Supervision | 25 March 2026 | Section 2 asks regulated entities to use automated tools for data tagging and classification. The tools identify, label and store customer data by sensitivity across in-house systems, cloud networks and third-party systems. The advisory has 12 sections, from governance to continuous monitoring | Secondary. MediaNama, `medianama.com/2026/04/223-rbi-data-protection-cybersecurity-guidelines-advisory-2026/`. Consentin, `consent.in/blog/rbi-customer-data-advisory`, which says RBI did not release it publicly |
| RBI (Commercial Banks – Cybersecurity, Technology: Risk, Resilience and Assurance Framework) Directions, 2026 | Master Direction, binding | Issued and effective 31 July 2026 | Consolidates RBI technology expectations into entity-wise directions. Regulated entities must re-map controls and update references to repealed circulars. [confirm the clause numbers on data classification and data leak prevention from the RBI text] | Primary, `rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13592`. Summary, KPMG India, September 2026 |
| DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 | Act and Rules, binding | Rules notified 13 November 2025, G.S.R. 846(E). Full obligations due about 13 May 2027 | Reasonable security safeguards for personal data. MeitY proposed moving the deadline to about November 2026 in a 23 January 2026 consultation. No gazette notification confirms the earlier date | Secondary. Seclore DPDP Rules guide. The AccuKnox DPDP blog, February 2026 |
| RBI draft Guidance on Regulatory Principles for Model Risk Management, 2026 | Draft guidance | Released 24 June 2026. Comments closed 24 July 2026 | Covers AI and ML models, third-party models included. Supports the AI data risk section, not the DSPM core | Primary, `rbi.org.in/Scripts/bs_viewcontent.aspx?Id=5089`. ## Older Frameworks Still Sit in the Console

AccuKnox maps these frameworks in the compliance module. They are useful as proof, but none of them
is new.

- RBI MD-ITF, the Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices,
  RBI/2023-24/107, 7 November 2023. [confirm whether the 31 July 2026 Directions supersede it for
  commercial banks]
- The RBI-UCB graded cyber security framework, levels I to IV.
- SEBI CSCRF, for SEBI regulated entities. Tier D, `docs/support-matrix/compliance-matrix.md`.
- CERT-In reporting, which the Indian public sector bank SBOM case study covers.

## The Page Leads With the Advisory, Then the Deadline

Suggested hero eyebrow: "RBI Advisory 3/2026 and DPDP Rules 2025". Suggested line under the H1: "An
RBI advisory now asks banks to tag and classify customer data with automated tools, and DPDP
obligations fall due by May 2027."

Do not quote the advisory text on a public page. RBI has not published it, and the copies online
come from third parties.
