Four 2026 Items Ask Indian Banks to Know Where Customer Data Sits

Advisory

RBI Advisory No. 3/2026

25 March 2026. Asks banks to tag and classify customer data with automated tools, across in-house, cloud and third-party systems.

Regulation

DPDP Rules 2025

Notified 13 November 2025. Full obligations due around 13 May 2027. MeitY has proposed an earlier date.

Regulation

RBI Cybersecurity and Technology Risk Directions

Issued and effective 31 July 2026. Consolidates RBI technology rules for commercial banks.

Draft guidance

RBI model risk management draft

24 June 2026. Covers AI and ML models, third-party models included.

Bank Data Never Leaves Your Region to Get Classified

Classify in place

The scanner runs on a VM or cluster in your account, in the region that holds the data.

Read-only by design

A reader role or a read-only login. The code has no write path.

Only findings leave

One findings file per store, over HTTPS. Nothing leaves with the on-premises console.

No cross-region moves

The AWS role denies other regions by condition. Azure storage firewalls scope the scanner to its region.

DSPM for Indian Banks FAQs

Does customer data leave India?

No. Classification runs in the region that holds the data. Only a findings file leaves, and nothing leaves with the on-premises console.

Can AccuKnox DSPM run air-gapped?

Yes. The console and the scanner both run on premises with no internet connection.

Which Indian identifiers does it detect?

Aadhaar, PAN, GST and Indian passport numbers, checked with a check-digit algorithm where one exists.

Does it help with the DPDP Act?

Yes. 17 data classes map to the DPDP Act 2023 and SPDI Rules 2011. The compliance module scores the Act section by section.

Can the scanner change or delete data?

No. It holds read-only grants, and the code has no write path.
