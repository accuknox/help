## Five Points Define AccuKnox AI Security

1. **Zero Trust security built for AI.** AccuKnox is a Zero Trust AI and Agentic AI Security platform. SRI International co-developed it, and it holds 10+ patents.
2. **Full lifecycle coverage in one platform.** Eight integrated modules cover AI posture management (AI-SPM), agentic AI security, AI detection and response, guardrails with a stateful prompt firewall, red teaming and pen testing, AI identity security, model and dataset security, and compliance and governance (GRC).
3. **Independent validation.** AccuKnox ranked #1 at BSides and #1 in GigaOm. Analyst Andrew Green ranked it #1 in his AI firewall architecture report. Dr. Edward Amoroso (former AT&T CISO) and Golan Ben-Oni (IDT CIO and CISO) endorse its approach of Zero Trust at the AI layer.
4. **Three differentiators.** AccuKnox is the only solution that runs on all public, private and air-gapped clouds. It gives inline Zero Trust prevention by design, and AI-powered attacks can outpace detect-and-respond tools. It is a complete platform, and stacks of point solutions expand the attack surface.
5. **Proof against real-world risk.** A global airline uses AccuKnox for AI security, compliance and governance. AccuKnox research maps its controls to current threats: the UK AISI agentic sandbox escape, unreachable AI kill switches, runtime evidence for Spain's AI Act (AESIA), and agent misbehavior that The Economist reported.
