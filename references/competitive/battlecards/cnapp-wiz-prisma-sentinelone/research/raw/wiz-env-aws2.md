## Overview

### Secure your AWS environment

Wiz is a premier cloud security partner for Amazon Web Services (AWS), providing a unified platform to secure everything organizations build and run on the world’s most comprehensive cloud. Built on AWS since its inception, Wiz is a **Diamond Technology Partner** and was named the **2024 AWS Marketplace Partner of the Year,** underscoring a deep, long-standing alliance dedicated to customer success and cloud-native innovation.

As a "Deployed on AWS" solution, Wiz provides seamless, agentless visibility across the **entire AWS ecosystem** in minutes. By leveraging the Wiz Security Graph—powered by AWS Neptune—Wiz correlates signals from native services like Amazon GuardDuty, AWS Security Hub, and Amazon Inspector to identify the **"toxic combinations"** that create real attack paths. From Amazon EKS and Lambda to sensitive data in Amazon S3 and RDS, Wiz delivers **100% coverage with zero impact on workload performance**, allowing Security, DevOps, and Platform teams to scale securely without operational friction.

### Securing AI Together

As organizations accelerate their AI journey, Wiz and AWS are **co-innovating** to secure the next generation of applications. Wiz integrates with **Amazon Bedrock** to provide automated, AI-driven remediation and visibility into AI pipelines, ensuring that rapid innovation is matched by robust guardrails. By centralizing risk across identity, vulnerabilities, and configurations, Wiz transforms complex cloud telemetry into a prioritized, actionable roadmap for remediation.

Together, Wiz and AWS empower the world’s largest enterprises to embrace a new operating model for cloud security—one built on trust, scale, and shared visibility.
