Integrate Prisma Cloud with your CI/CD runs in order to scan and detect misconfigurations in your infrastructure-as-code (IaC) files, vulnerabilities in your software composition analysis (SCA) packages, exposed secrets and license noncompliance in your code.

CI/CD Run integrations include:

- [Azure Pipelines](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-azure-pipelines)

- [AWS Code Build](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-aws-codebuild)

- [CircleCI](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-circleci)

- [Checkov](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-checkov)

- [GitHub Actions](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-github-actions)

- [GitLab Runner](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-gitlab-runner)

- [Jenkins](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-jenkins)

- [Terraform Cloud (Sentinel)](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-terraform-cloud-sentinel)

- [Terraform Cloud (Run Tasks)](https://docs.prismacloud.io/content-collections/application-security/get-started/connect-code-and-build-providers/ci-cd-runs/add-terraform-run-tasks)
