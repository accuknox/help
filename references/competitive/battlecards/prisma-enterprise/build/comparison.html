Why Customers Choose AccuKnox Over Prisma Cloud

Better

AccuKnox secures code, cloud, Kubernetes, data and AI in one platform, mapped to 33+ compliance frameworks. Its runtime engine, KubeArmor, is a CNCF Sandbox project with 2 million+ downloads.1,37

Faster

KubeArmor enforces policy inline at the kernel, so a disallowed process stops before it runs. At one of India's top 3 public sector banks, AccuKnox cut remediation time by 91% and false positives by 89%.24,46

Affordable

One AccuKnox platform includes SAST, DAST, DSPM and AI security. Prisma Cloud Enterprise Edition documents no native SAST or DAST, and Palo Alto Networks sells AI red teaming and prompt inspection separately as Prisma AIRS.48,50
