# End-of-Life Announcements

**Prisma Access Panorama Cloud Services Plugin End-of-Life & Updated Lifecycle Policy**

Effective September 21, 2026, Palo Alto Networks is announcing the End-of-Life (EOL) for Prisma Access Panorama Cloud Services Plugin version 6.0 and all prior versions, effective February 28, 2027.

Full support for legacy Cloud Services Plugin versions (6.0 and prior) will continue through February 27, 2027. New hotfix updates will no longer be generated for plugin versions 6.0 and lower, starting September 30, 2026. Customers currently running these older versions are required to upgrade to the latest supported plugin available in the [Customer Support Portal](https://support.paloaltonetworks.com/Support/Index).

Additionally, Palo Alto Networks is introducing a 18-Month Lifecycle Policy for all future releases of the Prisma Access Panorama Cloud Services Plugin.

**End of Life for Panorama-based Prisma Access multi-tenancy for New customers**

To ensure our customers and partners continue to benefit from the most advanced and scalable cloud management capabilities, we are evolving our approach to Prisma Access multi-tenancy.

- **Effective April 15, 2026**, Panorama-based Prisma Access multi-tenancy will no longer be available for new (greenfield) deployments.
- **No impact to existing customers**: Organizations currently using Panorama multi-tenancy may continue operating with full support, with no required changes to their environments or day-to-day operations.
- **Ongoing support commitment**: Existing deployments will remain fully supported. However, as innovation shifts toward next-generation platforms, certain features introduced after April 15, 2026, may be available exclusively through the newer Strata Cloud Manager (SCM) platform.
- **Recommendation**: We strongly recommend migrating from Panorama multi-tenancy to SCM multi-tenancy to take advantage of future enhancements and capabilities. Please contact your Palo Alto Networks account team for more details on the migration process.

For **new customers and partners onboarding new deployments**, we recommend **SCM multi-tenancy**. This platform delivers:

- Enhanced capabilities and faster access to new innovations
- Deeper integrations across the Strata portfolio
- A more intuitive, streamlined management experience designed for scale

By adopting SCM platform from day one, customers can take advantage of the latest functionality while simplifying operations and preparing for future growth.

This platform evolution reflects our continued commitment to our customers and partners, prioritizing operational simplicity, long-term scalability, and a future-ready cloud management experience.

Palo Alto Networks is announcing the End-of-Sale (EOS) and End-of-Life (EOL) for its Cloud Security Service Providers (“CSSP”) program. This decision is part of our ongoing effort to streamline our offerings and provide a more unified and scalable platform for our partners.

**Affected SKUs**

All PAN-VM-\*\* **SP**\*\* **-BND**-YU/MU/HU SKUs under CSSP program such as:

PAN-VM-100-SP-PREM-BND2-YU,

PAN-VM-50-SP-PREM-BND2-YU etc.

**Key Dates**

- End-of-Sale (EOS): April 1, 2026. After this date, no new CSSP licenses can be purchased.
- End-of-Life (EOL): October 1, 2026. On this date, all CSSP services will cease to be supported.

**Action Required for Partners**

To ensure a smooth transition and maintain uninterrupted service for your customers, all partners participating in the authorized CSSP program must migrate their licenses to either the new Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP) Pay-as-you-go (PAYG) SKU (reach out to [mssp\_payg\_support@paloaltonetworks.com](mailto:mssp_payg_support@paloaltonetworks.com)) or [Software NGFW Credits](https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/vm-series/11-1/vm-series-deployment/license-the-vm-series-firewall/software-ngfw).

The MSSP PAYG model provides a flexible, scalable, and consumption-based licensing structure that is better aligned with the needs of modern security service providers.

**End of Sale for Zycada Networks**

**March 07, 2024**

Effective March 7, 2024 Palo Alto Networks announces an End-of-Sale (EOS) and End-of-Life (EOL) for all Zycada Networks products. As part of the announcement, the EOS is effective immediately and no new orders or renewals for any Zycada products or services will be accepted, and Zycada will not be releasing any new features. For existing Zycada customers with a valid support contract or license in effect as of March 7, 2024, Zycada will honor all current subscription and support contracts through the end of the current term of such contracts. As of October 1, 2024, all Zycada products will be fully decommissioned and will no longer be supported.
