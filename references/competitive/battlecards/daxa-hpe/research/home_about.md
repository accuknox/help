# Built On One Idea: Control The Data AI Can Reach.

Daxa is the data control layer for agentic AI. We decide what agents may see and do with enterprise data, and enforce it in the infrastructure.

// our story

## Why DAXA Exists

Daxa started in 2022 with a simple observation: AI is only as safe as the data it can reach. While most of the industry worked on filtering what models said, we worked on what they could see. We built permission-aware data controls for AI apps and released Pebblo as open source so builders could have them from day one.

Then AI started to act. Agents now read, change and delete data across systems, at machine speed, with no person in the loop. The risk moved from what AI says to what AI does with your data.

In 2024 we rebuilt Daxa to enforce in the infrastructure itself, from network to endpoint, so agents can't route around it. Today Daxa is built into HPE AI Factory and Private Cloud AI, and we work with NVIDIA on agent governance.
