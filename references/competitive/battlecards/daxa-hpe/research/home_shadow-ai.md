// THE CHALLENGE

## The Shadow AI Dilemma

Employees are already using AI services. Without proper governance, your organization faces significant risks.

![data-leakage](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade48b2b240aebc635ee_7705b904c33c22cf2ebd8e9d4742ca16_data-leakage.png)

## Data Leakage

Sensitive company data, customer information, and intellectual property exposed to external AI services without oversight or control.

![shield with cross icon in it ](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade5815d643b435605ef_c06ea3652c885a622690adee88e33da1_lost-compliance.png)

## Lost Compliance

Unaudited AI interactions violate GDPR, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and ISO 42001 requirements, creating regulatory exposure.

![Lack of Visibility](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade51a250decb8494c84_c83ce1e57564b485a46d9a835c5f725d_Lack%20of%20Visibility.png)

## Lack of Visibility

Security teams have no insight into which AI services are being used, what data is shared, or who is accessing what.

![Prompt Injection Attacks](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade50cb9dd7866ad93ba_a1cd2bbc812592803838f489bb126d4a_Prompt%20Injection%20Attacks.png)

## Prompt Injection Attacks

Malicious prompts and indirect injection attacks can exfiltrate data or manipulate AI outputs without detection.

![Browser Plugin Nightmare](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade432f8aaf95084ce81_3d1f52cc7534dc95075bd7822f0de74a_Browser%20Plugin%20Nightmare.png)

## Browser Plugin Nightmare

Traditional solutions require plugins for every browser version, creating interoperability chaos and deployment headaches.

![Incomplete Coverage](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6368a330c6078773baa83c80/698dade4b86509a802c0e5c7_a0089526795bd89dbbba263a451808d2_Incomplete%20Coverage.png)

## Incomplete Coverage

Browser-based tools miss thick clients, mobile apps, CLI tools, and API integrations where AI usage is growing.
