## Key questions answered

##### What is the HPE Private Cloud AI Production Inference Platform?

Co-engineered with NVIDIA, HPE Private Cloud AI delivers a production-ready stack for inference, orchestration, and model development with a single console to deploy, monitor, and govern AI workloads, built-in security policies, and governed access to enterprise data, tools, and APIs.

##### How does HPE Private Cloud AI help move from pilots to production AI agents?

HPE Private Cloud AI provides a turnkey stack with validated blueprints, self-service tools, and a single console to deploy, orchestrate, and scale agentic workflows—skipping months of integration.

##### What does HPE Private Cloud include?

HPE Private Cloud includes a development environment, orchestration, and inference runtimes, along with governed connectors for data, tools, and APIs. A unified console lets you deploy, monitor, and manage AI lifecycles, while built-in policies, access control, logging, and audit capabilities provide governance across all workloads.

##### Can I use my own and third-party models, apps, and tools?

Yes, import proprietary, Hugging Face, or NVIDIA NIM models into a governed environment; connect enterprise data via low-code RAG pipelines and integrate custom apps without rebuilding stacks.

##### Why use HPE Private Cloud AI instead of only public cloud AI services?

Public cloud AI works well for trying ideas and handling occasional spikes, but 24/7 AI agents can quickly drive up usage-based costs because every interaction is metered. HPE Private Cloud AI gives you a dedicated platform close to your data, so you can keep AI costs more predictable while maintaining sovereignty and low latency for production workloads.

##### When is HPE Private Cloud AI a strong fit for customers?

Ideal for regulated industries (healthcare, finance, manufacturing) needing data residency, high-volume inference, or agentic AI on sensitive data—when pilots stall on security, scale, or cost.

##### What are common workloads for HPE Private Cloud AI?

Multi-agent orchestration (supply chain, customer service), real-time vision AI, grounded enterprise copilots and agents, and generative apps like fraud detection or predictive maintenance—all optimized for GPUs.

##### How does NVIDIA integration accelerate inference?

Co-engineered with NVIDIA, it leverages NIM microservices, Blackwell GPUs, and blueprints for low-latency, multi-node inference—delivering up to 30x throughput on enterprise workloads.
