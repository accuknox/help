## We’re here to answer your questions

[View Datasheet\\
![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67908b550d7a492979e824f6/6790c4b057585209b881c1ca_arrow-right.svg)](https://www.daxa.ai/glean#)

Daxa enforces permissions and governance policies at the data and retrieval layer, ensuring only authorized, role-appropriate context reaches the AI application or agent. Unauthorized access attempts can also be monitored and audited.

What if our existing applications already have permission issues?

Daxa adds an additional governance layer to your AI data flows. Its Safe Connectors capture existing permissions, while Safe Retriever applies identity, semantic, and policy controls before data reaches the AI model.

Can Daxa work with our existing AI applications and infrastructure?

Yes. Daxa can be integrated into existing RAG applications, AI applications, and agentic workflows through its SDK, APIs, or MCP integrations. It can be deployed within your existing environment so enterprise data remains under your control.

Does Daxa work with our existing LLMs and AI models?

Yes. Daxa is designed to sit across the AI data flow rather than lock you into a specific model. It can govern the data retrieved for your application and route inference through policy-approved models or adapters.

How does Daxa protect against sensitive data leakage?

Daxa classifies and tags enterprise data during ingestion, captures existing permissions, and enforces access controls at retrieval time. This ensures sensitive or restricted data is filtered before it reaches the AI application or model.
