Research Validation

User research validation plan

  • Test with four cohorts: CISO or sponsor, security analyst, cloud security architect, compliance owner.
  • Benchmark three tasks: identify top risk, prove RBI or DPDP control status, execute or route a safe remediation.
  • Measure time to first comprehension on the dashboard and time to root-cause understanding on a high-risk finding.
  • Capture whether the CNAPP plus DSPM differentiation is understood without verbal coaching.
  • Evaluate sponsor confidence before and after the executive summary and export flow.

Executive Demo

Recommended executive demo sequence

  1. Set the story in a telecom or regulated enterprise context with multi-cloud sensitive data and regulator pressure.
  2. Open the dashboard and show immediate posture comprehension: risk score, sensitive assets, over-permissioned identities, and compliance status.
  3. Trace one critical exposure from the triage board into asset detail, access graph, and lineage.
  4. Show the CNAPP x DSPM correlation screen to explain why the issue matters now, not just in theory.
  5. Open the compliance cockpit and prove RBI, DPDP, or TRAI evidence readiness.
  6. Close in the remediation center and the executive summary export to demonstrate both action and communication loops.
